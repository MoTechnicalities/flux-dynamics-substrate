## 2. Basic Q-Tribit Gates

These gates operate on the principle of **Polarity**, not just existence.

### 2.1 Polar NOT (Inversion)
Physically reverses the magnetic flux polarity.
*   `+1` $\to$ `-1` (Assertion becomes Negation)
*   `0` $\to$ `0` (Neutral remains Neutral)
*   `-1` $\to$ `+1` (Negation becomes Assertion)

### 2.2 Bipolar AND (Conjunction)
**Logic:** Conservative / Minimum Polarity.
*   `AND(+1, +1)` = `+1` (Resonance)
*   `AND(+1, -1)` = `-1` (Opposition $\to$ Negation wins)
*   `AND(0, -1)` = `-1` (Neutral + Negate $\to$ Negate)
*   `AND(-1, -1)` = `-1` (Shared Negation)

*Alternative (Multiplicative):* `AND(-1, -1) = +1` (Two negatives make a positive/double-negation).

### 2.3 Bipolar OR (Disjunction)
**Logic:** Optimistic / Maximum Polarity.
*   `OR(+1, -1)` = `+1` (Assertion wins over Negation)
*   `OR(0, +1)` = `+1` (Neutral + Assert $\to$ Assert)
*   `OR(-1, -1)` = `-1` (Shared Negation)

*Alternative (Summation):* `OR(+1, -1) = 0` (Cancellation/Annihilation).

### 2.4 Balanced XOR (Difference)
Measures the **topological distance** between states.
*   `XOR(+1, +1)` = `0` (No difference)
*   `XOR(-1, -1)` = `0` (No difference)
*   `XOR(+1, -1)` = `+1` (Maximal Difference)
*   `XOR(0, +1)` = `+1` (Deviation exists)
